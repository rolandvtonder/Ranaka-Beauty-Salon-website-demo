FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment at Ranaka Beauty Salon?+

Send us a WhatsApp on 075 284 7784 with the service and the day you would like. A 50% payment secures your booking.

Where is Ranaka Beauty Salon?+

We are at 15 Anderson Street, Goodwood, Cape Town, 7460.

Do your nail prices include soak-off?+

No — removing a previous gel or acrylic set is charged separately. Let us know when you book so we can plan the time.

What hair services do you offer?+

Braiding styles from wig lines and straight backs to freehand designs, amabengu and knowledge braids, plus hair washes.

Do you offer massage training?+

Yes. We run short massage courses, including a community course to help unemployed single mothers learn a skill and start their own business.
